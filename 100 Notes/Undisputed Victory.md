**The books**

- [[Arie de Geus, The Living Company]] A Shell study of firms over 100 years old.
- **David Landes, _Dynasties_** – the great family businesses, why some lasted and some rotted.
- **Nassim Taleb, _Antifragile_** – the Lindy effect (things that have survived long tend to keep surviving) and the mathematics of not dying.
- **Niall Ferguson, _The House of Rothschild_** – since you named them.
- **Jim Collins, _How the Mighty Fall_** – the five stages of decline. Better than his famous _Built to Last_, which aged badly because half its "eternal" companies stumbled.
- **Geoffrey West, _Scale_** – why companies die like animals while cities don't.
- **Ron Chernow, _Titan_** – Rockefeller. The rare case of total domination that survived by being broken apart.
