## 🚀 Final Project Info

The entire grade (100%) is based on a semester-long project done in pairs or individually. You will build a prompt-driven solution for a real-world need.

**Key Requirements:**

- **The Task:** Create a final product in one of 7 modalities: Text, Image, Video, Audio, Multimodal, Accessibility, or Application Prototype.
- **The Method:** You must compare a simple "baseline" prompt against your refined, engineered workflow to prove added value.
- **Evaluation:** Test your prompts using a structured method (e.g., 10-case test, human feedback, or blind testing).
- **Disclosure:** You must include a transparent AI-use disclosure explaining your process, human oversight, and data privacy compliance.

**Grading Breakdown:**

- **20%** – Prompt/workflow design & iteration
- **20%** – Evaluation and evidence of testing
- **20%** – Final product quality and audience fit
- **15%** – Responsible, inclusive, and transparent practice
- **10%** – Final presentation
- **10%** – Individual process record
- **5%** – Project concept and user fit
